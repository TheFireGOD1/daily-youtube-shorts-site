Personal creator tool

Daily YouTube Shorts

A private automation that turns recent, source-linked AI infrastructure stories into short Blender animations for one YouTube channel.

Videos are kept private unless the channel owner separately enables public publishing after YouTube API approval. Narration uses only a voice reference supplied by the channel owner.

The tool uses Google authorization only to check the connected channel and upload videos at the owner's request. It is not a public service and has no other users.

Operated by the owner of the connected YouTube channel. Updated October 13, 2026.
